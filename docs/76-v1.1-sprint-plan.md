# NovaDepot v1.1 Sprint Plan

> Version: v1.1
> Theme: Procurement and sales closure + operational essentials
> Branch: `develop/v1.1`

## 1. Summary

NovaDepot v1.1 upgrades v1.0 from a smart WMS console into a lightweight inventory, procurement, and sales system. The minimum business chain is:

`purchase -> inbound -> inventory -> outbound -> sales`

v1.1 remains a single-tenant runnable architecture. It must preserve v1.0 WMS, customer service, AI, Agent, knowledge base, RBAC, and audit behavior.

## 2. Global Limits

- Do not implement v1.2 DeepSeek production integration.
- Do not enable v2.0 multi-tenant runtime behavior.
- Do not add `TenantInterceptor`.
- Do not implement billing.
- Do not implement a full finance system.
- Do not implement procurement approval workflow.
- Do not implement purchase returns or sales returns.
- Do not implement invoice, contract, quotation, inquiry, or price comparison.
- Do not implement complex BPM.
- Do not break the v1.0 accepted WMS, CS, AI, Agent, knowledge, or audit paths.

## 3. Architecture Decisions

- `partners` is the v1.1 primary counterparty model for suppliers, customers, and mixed partners.
- Existing `suppliers` and `customers` tables remain for compatibility and are not deleted in v1.1 Sprint 1.
- Purchase and sales orders use `partner_id` as the v1.1 link field.
- Existing `tenant_id` behavior is preserved. New tables include `tenant_id`, but v1.1 does not enable tenant filtering or add `TenantInterceptor`.
- New backend code follows controller / service / repository / entity / request DTO structure.
- New frontend pages use Vue 3, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Naive UI, matching the current v1.0 frontend.

## 4. Four Sprint Plan

### Sprint 1: Partners + Basic Purchase/Sales Orders

1. Goal: Create the ERP foundation with counterparties, purchase orders, and sales orders.
2. Only do: partner CRUD, purchase CRUD, sales CRUD, `DRAFT / CONFIRMED / CANCELLED` state flow, permissions, audit logs, seed data, and minimum shared frontend components.
3. Do not do: inbound/outbound conversion, inventory linkage, receivables/payables, approval workflow, returns, invoices, contracts, or quotations.
4. Pages: `/erp/partners`, `/erp/purchases`, `/erp/sales`.
5. Backend modules: `partners`, `purchase`, `sales`, plus existing `auditlogs` and RBAC.
6. Tables: `partners`, `purchase_orders`, `purchase_order_items`, `sales_orders`, `sales_order_items`.
7. Permissions: `PARTNER_*`, `PURCHASE_*`, `SALES_*`; admin full, warehouse operates purchase and views sales, CS operates sales and views purchase, observer read-only.
8. Audit: create/update/disable partner; create/update/confirm/cancel purchase and sales orders.
9. Seed data: sample partners and sample draft/confirmed purchase and sales orders.
10. Tests: backend smoke/API tests, frontend ERP page smoke, permission checks, audit checks, v1.0 regression.
11. Rollback: hide ERP routes and remove new role grants; do not alter v1.0 WMS paths.
12. Acceptance: ERP pages usable, permissions enforced, audit visible, v1.0 regression green.

### Sprint 2: Procurement/Sales Connected to WMS

1. Goal: Connect purchase to inbound and sales to outbound.
2. Only do: create inbound draft from confirmed purchase, create outbound draft from confirmed sales, display source/target on WMS orders, update purchase/sales fulfillment status, block insufficient stock.
3. Do not do: complex split shipment UI or finance.
4. Pages: `/erp/purchases`, `/erp/sales`, `/wms/inbound`, `/wms/outbound`.
5. Backend modules: `purchase`, `sales`, `inboundorders`, `outboundorders`, `inventory`.
6. Tables: purchase/sales order items plus inbound/outbound source columns.
7. Permissions: conversion requires source order permission and target WMS create permission.
8. Audit: conversion and status linkage events.
9. Seed data: purchase-to-inbound and sales-to-outbound examples.
10. Tests: conversion, source display, stock shortage, WMS regression.
11. Rollback: disable conversion buttons while retaining base orders.
12. Acceptance: purchase and sales can produce WMS drafts and remain traceable.

### Sprint 3: Receivables/Payables + Stocktake

1. Goal: Add lightweight ledger visibility and stock count correction.
2. Only do: payable ledger, receivable ledger, payment/receipt registration, stocktake order, count entry, difference confirmation, inventory adjustment.
3. Do not do: full accounting, invoice, aging, automatic reconciliation, blind count, PDA, or cycle count.
4. Pages: `/erp/finance`, `/wms/stock-take`, dashboard summary cards.
5. Backend modules: `finance`, `stocktake`, `inventory`.
6. Tables: `payables`, `receivables`, `payments`, `stocktake_orders`, `stocktake_order_items`, `inventory_transactions`.
7. Permissions: finance admin/limited read, stocktake warehouse/admin, observer read-only.
8. Audit: ledger creation, payment, receipt, stocktake start, difference confirm, inventory adjustment.
9. Seed data: unpaid/partial/paid ledgers and stocktake examples.
10. Tests: ledger balances, payment/receipt status, stocktake inventory adjustment, permissions, audit.
11. Rollback: hide finance and stocktake routes.
12. Acceptance: ledger balances and stock adjustment are correct and auditable.

### Sprint 4: Operational Essentials Closure

1. Goal: Finish import, print, backup, tenant_id checks, data quality, E2E, and docs.
2. Only do: product/inventory/partner CSV import, inbound/outbound/picking print templates, backup script and record listing, tenant_id consistency checks, E2E and docs closure.
3. Do not do: Excel import, export reports, import rollback, label printers, custom print designer, batch print, auto restore, cloud backup, or incremental backup.
4. Pages: product, inventory, partners, inbound, outbound, backup settings.
5. Backend modules: `products`, `inventory`, `partners`, `backup`.
6. Tables: `import_error_reports`, `backup_records`, tenant_id/index checks for new tables.
7. Permissions: import permissions per module; backup admin only.
8. Audit: import report, backup trigger, optional print event.
9. Seed data: CSV templates and backup examples.
10. Tests: CSV valid/invalid rows, print QA, backup script, data quality, full E2E.
11. Rollback: hide new operational routes and keep data.
12. Acceptance: import, print, backup, data quality, and E2E pass.

## 5. Recommended Execution

Start with Sprint 1 because it defines the business data foundation for all later WMS linkage, finance, and operational closure work.

## 6. Sprint 1 Detailed Plan

1. Update docs first and freeze Sprint 1 decisions.
2. Add `partners` table, entity, mapper, controller, service, request DTO, and permissions.
3. Add purchase and sales list/detail/create/update/confirm/cancel APIs with item rewrite in draft status.
4. Write audit logs for partner, purchase, and sales mutations.
5. Add seed permissions, role grants, sample partners, and sample orders.
6. Add shared frontend components: page header, data table wrapper, search form, status badge, confirm dialog, empty state, and error state.
7. Add ERP service APIs and three pages: partners, purchases, sales.
8. Add ERP routes and side navigation entries based on permissions.
9. Run backend tests, frontend typecheck/build or E2E where available, and data quality checks.

## 7. Acceptance Criteria

- Admin users can manage partners and operate purchase/sales orders.
- Warehouse users can operate purchase orders and view sales orders.
- CS users can operate sales orders and view purchase orders.
- Observer can view ERP pages but cannot mutate.
- Audit center can filter partner, purchase, and sales mutations.
- Existing v1.0 WMS/CS/AI/Agent/knowledge tests do not regress.

## 8. Sprint 1 Closure Checklist

### Scope Guard

Sprint 1 closure remains limited to partner CRUD, purchase order CRUD, sales order CRUD, draft/confirmed/cancelled status flow, RBAC, audit, seed data, ERP pages, shared frontend primitives, and minimal E2E coverage.

Explicitly excluded from this closure: purchase-to-inbound, sales-to-outbound, receivables/payables, stocktake, CSV import, print templates, backup, tenant interceptor, tenant filtering, DeepSeek production integration, billing, v1.2, and v2.0 work.

### Shared Component Usage

- `/erp/partners`: `PageHeader`, `SearchForm`, `ErrorState`, `DataTable`, `StatusBadge`, `ConfirmDialog`, `EmptyState`.
- `/erp/purchases`: `PageHeader`, `SearchForm`, `ErrorState`, `DataTable`, `StatusBadge`, `ConfirmDialog`, `EmptyState`.
- `/erp/sales`: `PageHeader`, `SearchForm`, `ErrorState`, `DataTable`, `StatusBadge`, `ConfirmDialog`, `EmptyState`.

### Manual Acceptance Paths

- Admin: can enter `/erp/partners`, `/erp/purchases`, `/erp/sales`; can create/update partners and create/update/confirm/cancel purchase and sales orders; audit center can query `ERP_PARTNER`, `ERP_PURCHASE`, and `ERP_SALES`.
- Warehouse role: can operate purchase orders, can view sales orders, cannot mutate sales orders.
- CS role: can operate sales orders, can view purchase orders, cannot mutate purchase orders.
- Observer role: can view partners, purchase orders, and sales orders only; mutation APIs return 403.
- No-permission path: router redirects unauthorized ERP routes to `/access-denied`; API 403 emits the existing business permission message.
